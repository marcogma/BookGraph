# Summary

In Lima in the 1960s, the journalist Santiago Zavala ("Zavalita") runs into Ambrosio, his father's former chauffeur, and they spend four hours drinking at a cheap bar called La Catedral. Their conversation, cut into fragments and mixed with many other conversations and time periods, rebuilds Peru under General Manuel Odría's dictatorship (1948–1956). Santiago, from a rich family, rejected his father, Don Fermín, became a communist student, was arrested, and settled into a dull job at the newspaper *La Crónica*, married to a nurse. Cayo Bermúdez, Odría's security chief, runs repression and blackmail and keeps a mistress, Hortensia. Hortensia is murdered. Santiago finds out his father had a secret sexual relationship with Ambrosio, and that Ambrosio killed Hortensia because she was blackmailing Don Fermín. The book opens with Santiago's question, "¿En qué momento se había jodido el Perú?" ("At what moment had Peru fucked itself up?").

# Main characters

- **Santiago Zavala**: the journalist who keeps asking when he and Peru went wrong
- **Ambrosio Pardo**: his father's chauffeur
- **Don Fermín Zavala**: Santiago's father, a businessman tied to the regime
- **Cayo Bermúdez**: Odría's security chief
- **Hortensia (la Musa)**: Bermúdez's mistress, who is murdered
- **Amalia**: a servant, Ambrosio's lover
- **Queta**: a prostitute and Hortensia's friend
