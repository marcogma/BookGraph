# Notes

Haven't given this book its own dedicated exam yet, but it came up twice on its own while working through *The Meek One* and *The Idiot* — which tells me it's more load-bearing in my head than I gave it credit for at the time.

Reading *The Meek One*, it reminded me of a character explaining a philosophy that doesn't actually reflect in how he lives. Reading *The Idiot*, it's where the 2+2=4 idea comes from: you can hold something to be objectively true and still refuse it — "I don't think so" — and that refusal is what freedom actually is, not some kind of irrational chaos. Worth coming back to this one properly.
