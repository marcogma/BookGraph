# Summary

The second volume covers José López Portillo's presidency (1976–1982). It opens with the end of Luis Echeverría's term in 1976: the government-backed takeover of the newspaper *Excélsior* and the first devaluation of the peso in twenty-two years. Under López Portillo come the oil discoveries, borrowing, and the promise that Mexico would have to learn to "administer abundance", then the fall in oil prices, the 1982 debt crisis, and the nationalization of the banks. As in the first volume, he mixes in music, film, literature, and the counterculture of the period.

# Main characters

- **Luis Echeverría**: president 1970–1976, whose term ends as the book begins
- **José López Portillo**: president 1976–1982
