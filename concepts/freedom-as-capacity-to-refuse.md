# Definition

For Dostoevsky, freedom isn't irrationality — it's the capacity to refuse what is true or good, even something as certain as 2+2=4. A "rational paradise" (the crystal palace) is experienced as a prison precisely because it leaves no room to say "I don't think so." Marco drew this connection himself, unprompted, linking *The Idiot* back to *Notes from Underground*.
