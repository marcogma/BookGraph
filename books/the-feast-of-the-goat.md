# Summary

Three storylines. In 1996 Urania Cabral, a successful lawyer in New York, returns to Santo Domingo for the first time in thirty-five years to see her dying father, Agustín Cabral, once a top official of the dictator Rafael Trujillo. In May 1961 the aging Trujillo (nicknamed "the Goat") goes through his last day, humiliating his ministers and worried about his incontinence. On the same night a group of conspirators wait by the highway to ambush his car, and they kill him on May 30, 1961. Afterward the plotters are hunted down, and most are tortured and killed by Trujillo's son Ramfis and the secret police; General Pupo Román, who was supposed to take power, hesitates and is destroyed. The puppet president Joaquín Balaguer maneuvers into real power. At the end Urania tells her relatives what happened to her at fourteen: her father, having fallen out of favor, gave her to Trujillo to win him back, and Trujillo, impotent, raped her with his hand. Nuns helped her leave for the United States.

# Main characters

- **Urania Cabral**: the lawyer who returns to Santo Domingo
- **Agustín Cabral**: her father, a former Trujillo minister
- **Rafael Leónidas Trujillo**: the dictator
- **Antonio Imbert**: one of the assassins, one of only two who survive
- **Antonio de la Maza**: an assassin whose brother Trujillo had killed
- **Amadito García Guerrero**: a young officer in Trujillo's guard, among the assassins
- **Salvador Estrella Sadhalá**: a devout Catholic assassin
- **Johnny Abbes García**: head of the secret police
- **Joaquín Balaguer**: Trujillo's mild-mannered puppet president
- **Ramfis Trujillo**: the dictator's son, who takes revenge on the plotters

# Historical & cultural context

See [[the-latin-american-boom]], which includes Vargas Llosa's life. Trujillo ruled the Dominican Republic from 1930 to 1961. In 1937 his army massacred thousands of Haitians along the border (the Parsley massacre). The assassination and the people in it are real; Urania and her father are fictional. Balaguer was later president of the country for more than twenty years.
