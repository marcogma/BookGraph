# Summary

A knight devoted to doing good deeds (fighting dragons, saving damsels) spends so much time in his shining armor that he never takes it off. His wife Juliet and son Christopher can no longer see his face. When he tries to take it off, he finds it is stuck. He goes looking for the wizard Merlin, who sends him on the Path of Truth with a squirrel and a pigeon named Rebecca as companions. He has to pass through three castles. In the Castle of Silence he must listen to himself. In the Castle of Knowledge he learns about his need for approval and his failure to love. In the Castle of Will and Daring he faces the Dragon of Fear and Doubt. Each time he cries for real, his tears rust the armor and pieces fall away. At the Summit of Truth he lets go of everything he thought he knew, and the last pieces of armor fall away.

# Main characters

- **The knight**: the hero trapped in his own armor
- **Merlin**: the wizard who guides him
- **Squirrel and Rebecca the pigeon**: his companions on the path
- **Juliet and Christopher**: his wife and son

# Historical & cultural context

Robert Fisher (1922–2008) was an American writer of comedy for radio and television (he wrote for Groucho Marx, George Burns and others). The book came out in 1987 and became a long-running bestseller in Spanish translation, often used in therapy and personal growth groups in Latin America.
