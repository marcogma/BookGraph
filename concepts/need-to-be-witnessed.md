title: Need to be witnessed
---

# Definition

Distinguishes characters who need an audience for their suffering or transformation to mean something, from characters whose acts need no witness at all. The needy ones narrate, over-explain, or stage themselves; the others just act. Marco originated this throughline himself across the discussion of *The Meek One* and *The Idiot* — Claude only named it.

# Appears in

- [[the-meek-one]] — the pawnbroker over-explains the duel and his own restraint; his compulsive self-narration is what makes his "tenderness" suspect as genuine love rather than a performance of it. The meek one herself narrates nothing and needs no audience for her jump — total privacy is part of what makes it a real act of freedom.
- [[the-idiot]] — Ippolit reads a full confessional essay aloud *before* his failed suicide attempt (misremembered by Marco as a forgotten piece of armor — it's a forgotten percussion cap). He needs to be watched for his death to carry meaning; when the attempt fails, it reads as staged rather than as dignified failure.

# Related concepts
