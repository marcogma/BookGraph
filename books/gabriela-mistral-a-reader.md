# Summary

An anthology of Mistral's poetry and some prose, drawing on her main collections: *Desolación* (1922), which includes the "Sonetos de la muerte" written after the suicide of a young man she had loved; *Ternura* (1924), lullabies and rounds for children; *Tala* (1938); and *Lagar* (1954). Her recurring subjects are motherhood (she had no children), children, death, God, the Chilean landscape, and the Indigenous and rural people of Latin America. The prose pieces, or *recados*, are short essays and portraits.
