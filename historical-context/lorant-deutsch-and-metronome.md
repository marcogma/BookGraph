# What happened

Lorànt Deutsch (born 1975) is a French actor. *Métronome* (2009), his popular history of Paris organized around metro stations, one per century, became a large bestseller and was adapted for television on France 5. The books were attacked by some historians. In *Les Historiens de garde* (2013), William Blanc, Aurore Chéry, and Christophe Naudin criticized it for factual errors and for a nostalgic, Catholic and monarchist view of French history; Deutsch has said publicly that he has royalist sympathies. Readers who enjoyed the stories may want to check specific claims against academic histories.
