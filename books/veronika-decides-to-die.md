# Summary

Veronika, a twenty-four-year-old librarian in Ljubljana, Slovenia, has a normal, comfortable life and decides to die because nothing will ever change and the world is getting worse. She takes an overdose of sleeping pills and wakes up in Villete, a private mental hospital. Dr. Igor tells her the overdose damaged her heart and she has only about a week to live. Knowing she is going to die, she stops caring what people think and starts doing what she wants: she plays the piano at night, has outbursts of anger, and masturbates in front of a silent schizophrenic patient, Eduard. She becomes friends with Zedka, who is being treated for depression, and Mari, a former lawyer with panic attacks, and she falls in love with Eduard. Dr. Igor's diagnosis was a lie: he is testing a theory that awareness of death makes people value life. Veronika and Eduard escape together for one night in the city. The book ends with them alive, and Veronika does not know her heart is fine.

# Main characters

- **Veronika**: the young woman who attempts suicide
- **Dr. Igor**: the director of Villete
- **Zedka**: a patient being treated for depression
- **Mari**: a former lawyer with panic disorder
- **Eduard**: a schizophrenic patient, son of a diplomat, who wanted to be a painter

# Historical & cultural context

Published in 1998. Coelho (born 1947) appears as a character in the book. As a teenager in Rio de Janeiro in the 1960s, he was committed to a psychiatric hospital by his parents three times and given electroshock; the book draws on that. Villete is fictional, but Slovenia had only recently become independent (1991), and the novel mentions it.
