# Summary

The ghost of King Hamlet of Denmark appears to his son, Prince Hamlet, and tells him he was poisoned by his brother Claudius, who has taken the throne and married Hamlet's mother, Gertrude. Hamlet pretends to be mad while he decides what to do, and rejects Ophelia, daughter of the king's adviser Polonius. He has traveling actors perform a play showing a similar murder, and Claudius's reaction convinces him. He finds Claudius praying and does not kill him, so as not to send him to heaven. In his mother's room he stabs Polonius, hidden behind a curtain. Claudius sends Hamlet to England with Rosencrantz and Guildenstern to be killed; Hamlet switches the letters and returns. Ophelia goes mad and drowns. Her brother Laertes wants revenge, and Claudius arranges a fencing match with a poisoned sword and a poisoned cup. In the final scene Gertrude drinks the poison, Laertes and Hamlet wound each other with the poisoned blade, Laertes confesses, and Hamlet kills Claudius before dying in Horatio's arms. The Norwegian prince Fortinbras arrives and takes the throne.

# Main characters

- **Hamlet**: the prince of Denmark
- **Claudius**: his uncle, the new king and murderer
- **Gertrude**: Hamlet's mother, now Claudius's wife
- **The Ghost**: Hamlet's father
- **Polonius**: the king's adviser, killed by Hamlet
- **Ophelia**: Polonius's daughter, loved and rejected by Hamlet
- **Laertes**: Polonius's son
- **Horatio**: Hamlet's loyal friend
- **Rosencrantz and Guildenstern**: old school friends used by Claudius
- **Fortinbras**: prince of Norway

# Historical & cultural context

See [[elizabethan-theatre-marlowe-and-shakespeare]]. The edition I logged is Kenneth Branagh's book of his 1996 film: the screenplay, his introduction, and a diary of the shoot. The film used the full text, combining the 1623 First Folio with passages from the Second Quarto, and runs about four hours. Branagh set it in the 19th century and filmed the exteriors at Blenheim Palace; he played Hamlet, with Derek Jacobi as Claudius, Julie Christie as Gertrude and Kate Winslet as Ophelia.
