# What happened

José Agustín (José Agustín Ramírez Gómez, 1944–2024) published his first novel, *La tumba*, in 1964, at about twenty, in a series run by Juan José Arreola; he had begun it at sixteen. In 1971 the critic Margo Glantz grouped it, with *De perfil* (1966) and work by Gustavo Sainz and Parménides García Saldaña, as "literatura de la Onda". Its marks were young urban narrators, slang, rock and roll, sex, and drugs, written against the solemn national literature of the time. Agustín disliked the label.

In 1970 he was arrested for marijuana possession and spent months in Mexico City's Lecumberri prison, where he wrote. He later wrote film scripts, taught in the United States, and settled in Cuernavaca, Morelos. His three-volume *Tragicomedia mexicana* (1990–1998) is a popular history of Mexico from 1940 to 1994, written in his informal style. He died in Cuernavaca in January 2024.
