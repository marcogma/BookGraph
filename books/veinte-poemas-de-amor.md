# Summary

Twenty love poems and a final "desperate song", written by Neruda at nineteen as a student in Santiago. The poems describe a love affair through the body of the woman and the landscape of southern Chile: sea, rain, forest. They move from desire to loss and memory. Poem 20 opens "Puedo escribir los versos más tristes esta noche" and contains the line "Es tan corto el amor, y es tan largo el olvido". Poem 15 begins "Me gustas cuando callas porque estás como ausente". The closing "Canción desesperada" is a farewell.

# Historical & cultural context

See [[chilean-poetry-mistral-and-neruda]]. Published by Nascimento in Santiago in 1924. Neruda later said the poems were about two women, whom he called Marisol (a girl from Temuco) and Marisombra (a student in Santiago); biographers identify the second as Albertina Azócar.
