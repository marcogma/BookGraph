# Summary

A short first-person novel narrated by Gabriel Guía, a sixteen-year-old from a wealthy Mexico City family who wants to be a writer. He is bored and arrogant, despises his school and his parents' world, drinks, drives, goes to parties, and has a series of sexual relationships, including one with a cousin. He treats people carelessly and feels empty. The novel ends with him alone, putting a gun to his head, and the title refers to that ending.

# Main characters

- **Gabriel Guía**: the teenage narrator
