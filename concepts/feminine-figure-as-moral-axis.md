# Definition

Marco's own pattern-spot: in Dostoevsky's shorter fiction, a female character is the axis the male narrator's crisis or transformation turns on, without herself self-justifying or narrating her own meaning.

# Appears in

- [[the-meek-one]] — the title character
- [[dream-of-a-ridiculous-man]] — the girl who gives the narrator his first real reason to act
- [[the-idiot]] — Nastasya Filippovna
