# Summary

A novel for young readers (1992). A group of children from Mexico City spending time in Tepoztlán, Morelos, go into the Tepozteco hill and find a hidden world where they meet figures from pre-Hispanic myth, including Tepoztécatl, the local god of pulque. Through the adventure they learn about Mexico's Indigenous past and its relationship to the modern country. (I could not confirm the children's names, so this summary stays general.)
