# Summary

In a Caribbean port city around 1880–1930, the young telegraph clerk Florentino Ariza falls in love with Fermina Daza and courts her through letters. Her father sends her away, and when she returns she suddenly rejects Florentino and marries Dr. Juvenal Urbino, a modern, well-traveled doctor who fights cholera. Florentino waits fifty-one years, nine months and four days, while working his way up in a river shipping company and having 622 affairs, which he records in notebooks; one of them, with the teenage América Vicuña, his ward, ends with her suicide. Urbino dies falling from a ladder while trying to catch his parrot. At the funeral Florentino declares his love again. Fermina throws him out but later accepts his letters, and the two old people go on a river cruise together. To avoid other passengers, the captain raises the yellow cholera flag, and the boat goes up and down the river with no plan to stop, "forever".

# Main characters

- **Florentino Ariza**: the telegraph clerk, then shipping executive, who waits half a century
- **Fermina Daza**: the woman he loves, strong-willed and practical
- **Dr. Juvenal Urbino**: her husband, a respected doctor who modernizes the city
- **Lorenzo Daza**: Fermina's father, a mule trader who wants her to marry up
- **Tránsito Ariza**: Florentino's mother
- **América Vicuña**: the young relative placed in Florentino's care, whom he seduces
