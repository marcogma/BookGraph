# Summary

A popular history of Paris in twenty-one chapters, one per century from the 1st to the 21st, each tied to a metro station near a place where that century left a mark. It starts with the Gallic Parisii and Roman Lutetia on the Île de la Cité, and goes through Saint Denis and Saint Genevieve, the Merovingians and Capetians, Philippe Auguste's wall, the Templars, the Hundred Years' War, the Wars of Religion and Henri IV, Louis XIV, the Revolution, Napoleon, Haussmann's rebuilding, the Commune, the World Wars and the present. Deutsch writes as a guide walking with the reader, mixing history with anecdotes about the places you can still see.
