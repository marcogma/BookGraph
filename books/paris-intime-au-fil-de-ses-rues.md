# Summary

A follow-up to *Métronome* (listed on Goodreads as *Métronome* #2). Instead of following the metro, Deutsch walks through Paris street by street and tells the history behind street names, buildings, and lesser-known corners: who the streets are named after, what happened in them, and what traces remain.
