# Historical & cultural context

Bulgakov wrote this novel in secret between 1928 and 1940, under Stalin-era Soviet censorship that had already banned his other major works and plays — in 1930, in despair, he burned an early draft himself (the novel's own famous line, "manuscripts don't burn," is a direct response to that act). He continued dictating revisions to his wife even as he was dying, and never saw it published: it first appeared, still heavily censored, in a Moscow literary magazine in 1966–67, more than 25 years after his death in 1940.
